Knight Character | Pixel Heroes Vol.1 by elesrech
==============================================

Contents
------------------

This asset pack features a fully animated knight character designed for platformers and action games!
The pack includes 11 animations (67 frames):

	-Idle (9 frames)
	-Run (8 frames)
	-Stop (3 frames)
	-Jump (8 frames)
	-Land (2 frames)
	-Attack 1 (with and without VFX) (6 frames)
	-Attack jump 1 (with and without VFX) (6 frames)
	-Dash (3 frames)
	-Hurt 1 (4 frames)
	-Hurt 2 (6 frames)
	-Death (12 frames)
	
	
Technical details.
------------------

- Character size: approximately 24x24.
- Canvas size: 80x80.
- Format: PNG files, provided both in spritesheets and individual frames. 
- Outline version only.
