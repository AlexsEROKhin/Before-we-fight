## Warning ring

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: NEW warning marker sprite for a simple 2D pixel-art forest Trapper enemy. Image 1 is only a palette/context reference for the resin color, NOT an edit target. Top-down view of a single small flat circular trap target on the ground: an unmistakable hollow ring made of broken amber-gold pixel segments, with four slightly brighter cardinal ticks and a dark transparent center. Symmetric, readable at native 32x32 pixels, diameter about 26 pixels in a 32x32 canvas. Limited palette: dark brown outline, muted amber, bright golden warning pixels; at most 5 flat opaque colors. Intentional chunky pixel clusters; hard square pixels, no antialiasing, blur, gradient, glow, shadow, texture, runes, lettering, character, floor or extra shapes. Genuine fully transparent background outside and inside the ring. This is a gameplay warning, not a decorative illustration.

## Active puddle

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: NEW active sticky resin trap sprite for a simple top-down 2D pixel-art game. Image 1 is the old tiny resin sprite, used ONLY as amber palette reference. Design one circular, shallow sticky puddle viewed directly from above, matching a 32x32 warning ring. Diameter about 26 pixels within a 32x32 transparent square. Uneven dark-brown edge and mostly filled muted amber resin surface, with only two or three brighter blocky glints so it clearly differs from the hollow warning ring. Simple and humble like an ordinary creep effect, no large glow. Limited palette at most 5 flat opaque colors, hard square pixels, no antialiasing, blur, gradient, shadow, lettering, runes, character, floor, decorations or extra objects. Genuine transparent background outside the puddle. Readable after nearest-neighbor reduction to 32x32.
